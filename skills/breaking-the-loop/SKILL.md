# selfdriven.you — The Loop

A framework page for **breaking the negative feedback loop of the anxious mind**.
The central metaphor: anxiety is a *circuit*, not an argument. You don't win it —
you break it at one point and the rest loses its charge.

## The Framework (this is the core — reproduce it faithfully)

### The model: anxiety runs on feedback

A worried thought raises an alarm → the body believes it and fires up → the mind
reads the racing body as *proof* the danger is real → it thinks harder → the
behaviour it demands (avoid, check, reassure, scroll) feels like relief but quietly
teaches the brain the threat was genuine → round again. Each lap makes the next feel
more true.

Represent the loop as four nodes on a ring, feeding forward:
**THOUGHT → BODY → FEELING → BEHAVIOUR → (back to THOUGHT).**
The node colours cycle the four mode hues for visual rhythm only — they are **not**
the mode mapping. The mapping lives on the *exits*, below.

Two lines that must survive any rewrite:

- **The trap:** you try to out-think it. But you can't argue your way out of a loop
  from inside it — every rebuttal is another lap.
- **The move:** you don't calm the whole loop at once. You break the circuit at
  **one** point, and the rest loses its charge.

And the release valve: **one clean break is enough. Take whichever exit is closest
to hand.** Never imply the reader must do all four — that recreates the pressure the
page is trying to relieve.

### The four exits (mode mapping — keep it honest)

Each mode cuts the loop at a different link. This mapping is deliberate; keep it.

| Exit | Mode | Colour | Cuts the loop at | Essence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Chill** | violet `--chill-bright` | the **body** (nervous system) | pause · breathe · reset |
| 02 | **Curious** | amber `--curious` | the **thought** (fusion) | explore · ask · learn |
| 03 | **Caring** | rose `--caring` | the **inner voice** (self-criticism) | listen · hold space |
| 04 | **Constructive** | mint `--constructive` | the **behaviour** (avoidance) | build · ship · move |

Each exit = a *why* (one paragraph on which link it cuts and how) + three concrete
micro-moves with short bold keys. The moves as shipped:

- **Chill — settle the body first.** The loop lives in the nervous system before
  logic; a body in alarm makes every thought feel truer. → *Long exhale* (in 4,
  out 6, one minute — a longer out-breath signals the emergency is over) · *Orient*
  (name what you see: "I'm here, and here is safe") · *Move* (stand, shake out hands,
  walk — discharge the fight-or-flight charge).
- **Curious — question the thought.** An anxious thought arrives dressed as a fact;
  curiosity looks *at* it, not *through* it. → *Name the story* ("I'm having the
  thought that…") · *Ask better* (fact or forecast? what would I tell a friend?
  likely outcome, not worst?) · *Zoom out* (will this matter in a week? a year?).
- **Caring — soften the voice.** The harsh "you should be handling this better"
  voice feeds the loop; caring turns the kindness you'd give anyone else back on
  yourself. → *Kind words* ("this is hard, and I'm doing my best in it") · *Let it be*
  (allowing a feeling ≠ agreeing with it; "it will pass") · *Reach one* (say it out
  loud to someone safe — naming it to a human shrinks it).
- **Constructive — take one small step.** Anxiety's instruction is *avoid*; obeying
  tightens the loop. → *Tiny action* (smallest first move, not the whole scary thing)
  · *Drop one prop* (skip one checking / over-preparing / reassurance behaviour once)
  · *Next ordinary thing* (wash the cup, take the walk — life is still running).

### The 4-minute reset (the in-the-moment protocol)

For when the reader is mid-spiral and can't hold the whole framework. Five steps:
1. **Name it** (10s) — "I'm in the loop", out loud if possible.
2. **Breathe** (60s) — in 4, out 6.
3. **Land** (60s) — 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you can touch.
4. **Question one thought** (60s) — "Is this a fact, or a fear?"
5. **One small move** (60s) — tiniest action toward, not away.

Close with permission, not a target: *you don't have to feel great — you just have
to break the circuit once.*

## Wellbeing & Safety Framing — NON-NEGOTIABLE

This page sits on sensitive ground. The safety framing is part of the product, not a
disclaimer bolted on. It ships in every version.

1. **Position as everyday spirals, not treatment.** The page is a framework for the
   anxious loops everyone gets caught in — never a diagnosis, therapy, or substitute
   for care. Say so plainly.
2. **Always include the "When it's more than a loop" section** (rose-tinted `.help`
   card). It must: name the signs that mean *bring someone in* — anxiety that's
   **constant**, **shrinking your life**, or that **won't break** no matter what;
   reframe that as a signal, **not a willpower failure**; note anxiety is highly
   treatable and you don't have to earn the help; and point to a doctor / mental-health
   professional.
3. **Crisis pointer, done carefully.** Include: local emergency number, a crisis line
   in the reader's country, and `findahelpline.com` as the international list. Do **not**
   guarantee confidentiality or specific outcomes (they vary). Do **not** name or
   describe any method of self-harm anywhere on the page.
4. **No discomfort-as-coping techniques.** Never suggest pain, cold shock, or sensory
   jolt as regulation tools (no ice cubes, rubber-band snapping, cold plunges framed as
   shock). Stick to breath, grounding/orienting, gentle movement, self-compassion, and
   small approach behaviour. These are safe and evidence-informed.
5. **Keep the breathing gentle, never competitive.** No "beat your time", no target
   counts to push against, no numeric goals that could feed disordered striving. The
   pacer paces; it does not score.
6. **Tone stays warm and non-alarming.** The page is a calm room, not a warning label.
   The honest note lands *because* the rest is gentle.

## Voice & Copy

- Second person, short, direct, warm — the selfdriven house voice ("Stop Your Autopilot").
- Frame moves as *offers*, not orders. The reader is already under pressure.
- Bold the load-bearing half of a sentence, not the whole thing.
- Metaphor spine is electrical/mechanical: *loop, circuit, exit, break-in, charge* —
  keep it consistent; don't drift into "journey" or "battle" language.
- Never pathologise the reader or imply the loop is their fault.

## Page-Specific Components

Pull cursor, glow blobs, scroll-reveal, nav, hero-pill, mode-pill, and all tokens from
the `selfdriven-you` skill. The components below are unique to this page.

### Loop diagram (inline SVG)

A ~260×260 ring: dashed circle, four clockwise arcs with an arrowhead `marker`, four
coloured node dots at N/E/S/W labelled THOUGHT / BODY / BEHAVIOUR / FEELING in JetBrains
Mono, and "THE LOOP" centred. Node colours cycle the mode hues for rhythm only. Keep
labels legible on `--bg`; keep stroke greys in the `--muted`/`--muted2` range.

### Breathing pacer

- Orb scales between **1 (inhale)** and **.55 (exhale)**; idle rest at **.6**.
- Timing: **inhale 4s, exhale 6s**, driven by JS `setTimeout` with matching CSS
  `transition-duration` so the label stays in sync. Label cycles
  `breathe in` → `breathe out`; idle = `ready`.
- One toggle button: "Start breathing" ⇄ "Stop". Stopping clears timers and eases the
  orb back to rest.
- Copy: "In for four, out for six. Follow the orb until your shoulders drop." Gentle,
  never a target.

### Map your loop (reflection tool — mirrors the-quiet-mirror pattern)

Optional, private, on-device. Five fields, each with a label + hint:

| id | Label | Prompts for |
|---|---|---|
| `f-trigger` | What set it off? | the trigger |
| `f-thought` | The thought | what the loop is saying |
| `f-body` | Where you feel it | the body |
| `f-avoid` | What it wants you to do | the avoidance |
| `f-exit` | Your break-in | which exit + the one small move |

Persistence (follow the ecosystem rules exactly):
- **Own IndexedDB database** — `selfdriven-loop`, store `state`, single key `current`.
  Each page gets its own DB to avoid version conflicts. Never reuse another page's DB.
- **Debounced autosave** (~450ms) on input; stamp `_updated` ISO on write.
- **localStorage fallback** under the same key `selfdriven-loop` if IndexedDB fails,
  on both read (boot rehydrate) and write.
- **Markdown export** → `my-loop-YYYY-MM-DD.md`, Blob download, one `##` section per
  field (empty → `—`), closing with the "one clean break is enough" line. Date the file.
- **Clear** button: `confirm()` first, wipe fields + stored copy, flash "cleared".
- **Privacy line** under the tool: stored locally only, nothing uploaded, no one else
  can see it. This must be true — keep it client-side.

When editing the tool's `<script>` near closing `</script>` tags with `str_replace`,
verify open/close balance to avoid syntax errors. Wire any new field through **all**
layers: field markup → `FIELDS`/`LABELS` arrays → autosave collect → boot hydrate →
markdown export. A field added to only one layer is a bug.
