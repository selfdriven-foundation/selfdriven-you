the trade

Plugged in, tuned out.

Every device promises connection and delivers contact. Contact is cheap, constant and shallow. Connection is rare, slow and needs your whole face in the room.

What being plugged in gives you

  • A hundred people's highlights, none of their company.
  • The feeling of keeping up, and the fatigue of never catching up.
  • Half-attention to the person in front of you, and half-attention back.
  • Reactions instead of responses. Likes instead of listening.

What unplugging gives back

  • Eye contact that lasts long enough to mean something.
  • Silence that gets filled by the other person, not the phone.
  • The second question, the one you only think of when you're not elsewhere.
  • Being remembered as someone who was actually there.

You can't be fully with someone while you're partly somewhere else. Unplugging isn't a detox. It's the door.

four ways in

Connection has modes too.

Each of the 4Cs is a different way of being with a person. Pick the one that fits the moment, put the phone somewhere it can't reach you, and go in through that door.

Curious

ask · notice · learn them

Connection starts with interest. Ask the question you'd normally assume the answer to. Find out something you didn't know about someone you've known for years.

UNPLUGGED PRACTICEAsk one person "what's something you've been thinking about lately?" and follow it for three questions without offering your own thing.

Caring

listen · hold space · stay

Caring is attention before it's anything else. You can't listen with one eye on a notification. Put the device face down, or better, in another room.

UNPLUGGED PRACTICEHave one conversation this week with nothing in your hand. When it goes quiet, let it. Wait for them to fill it.

Constructive

make · fix · move together

Shoulder to shoulder beats face to face for a lot of people. Cook, build, garden, walk somewhere with a purpose. Hands busy, phones away, talk arrives on its own.

UNPLUGGED PRACTICEDo one job with someone instead of for them. Shared work is a conversation that doesn't need a topic.

Chill

be near · no agenda

Some of the best connection is just proximity with no plan. A cup of something. A porch. Two people in the same room not performing for anyone.

UNPLUGGED PRACTICESit with someone for twenty minutes with no screen between you and nothing you have to talk about.
make it real

Pick a person. Pick a window.

Intentions evaporate. Plans get kept. Name who, when and how you'll unplug for them, then come back and mark it done. It stays on this device only.
