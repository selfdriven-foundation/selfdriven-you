Chapter I · Before

Human speed

The world moved at the pace of a posted letter, a bank that shut at four, a dial tone that made you wait. Slack was built into the day.

In 1983, as quartz gutted Swiss watchmaking, Switzerland answered not with precision but with plastic, colour and play — the Swatch. Time became something you wore by mood, swapped, and threw away.

Chapter V · 1984 + 42

The answer we built

Orwell named a year. Douglas Adams named a number — 42 — the Answer to Life, the Universe and Everything, computed across seven and a half million years, by a machine, for a species that had forgotten the Question.
